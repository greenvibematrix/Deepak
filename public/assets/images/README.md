# Image Assets Guide for Sayandana's Birthday Website

This directory contains the visual art assets for the interactive story.

### Included Assets:

1. **`sayandana-cartoon.png`**
   - **Purpose**: Sayandana's cartoon illustration shown in Scene 2 (Birthday Reveal).
   - **Recommended specs**: Square aspect ratio (1:1), e.g., 800x800 px or 1000x1000 px, PNG or JPG.
   - **How to replace**: Replace this file with an illustrated/cartoon image of Sayandana that you have permission to use.
   - **Important**: Do not scrape or use unauthorized photos from social media.

2. **`magical-girl.png`**
   - **Purpose**: The cute Kerala-inspired fantasy birthday guardian/genie character who guides Sayandana through the three wishes.
   - **Details**: Adorned with jasmine flowers (*mulla poo*), traditional cream & gold Kasavu attire, crescent moon hair accessory, and glowing magical sparkles.

3. **`kerala-lamp.png`**
   - **Purpose**: The traditional glowing Kerala bronze oil lamp (*Nilavilakku*) nestled among fragrant jasmine flowers in the enchanted night garden.

All images are dynamically styled with CSS borders, subtle Kasavu gold accents, and glowing shadow effects in the UI.
